## Reconciliation and claim boundaries

- **Winter Weapon:** 31 July email reported 45 qualified (2 bottles, 43 shorts). The 3 August email reported 60 (3 bottles, 57 shorts), followed by Mel’s seven-bottle allocation correction. The 6 August thread moved from 69 confirmed shorts sizes plus two pending to 70 confirmed plus one pending. These categories and versions must not be combined into a single final reward total. September handover does not prove collection.
- **Price-rise mapping:** 352 mapped records on 14 August and 354 on the tracker refreshed 20 August are different snapshots. The Summary’s adult out-of-agreement count of 115 at a $5 change does not reconcile with its $650 uplift figure. Do not use the aggregate projected uplift until reviewed. A planned 21 September change is not realised revenue as of 15 September.
- **Fee export:** Monthly pricing and missing exported fields required interpretation and Clubworx checks. Twelve blank fees were not twelve unpaid accounts; no recovered revenue is claimed.
- **Engagement reporting:** The 28 August email referred to a 16 August snapshot with the 23 August data missing. Its 282 base and category counts 61 + 115 + 105 = 281 did not reconcile; an adult sleeper figure also conflicted with narrative wording. Those numbers are excluded from the review’s achievement claims.
- **Coach data:** A reviewed sheet header selected 11 September in day view. Individual ratings are not reproduced and a daily view is not described as a weekly outcome.
- **Meta Ads:** $2,995 / 78 is approximately $38.40 per reported lead. The source rounds to $38. The comparison with $46 is source-reported; spend-to-revenue attribution and lead qualification are not established here.
- **Business growth:** Weekly membership, cancellation and rollover outcomes are team/business results. Reporting support is Ram’s contribution; causality is not assigned to him alone.
- **Academy:** Resource delivery and positive passport feedback do not establish complete programme launch or final approval. Mel provided the programme vision.
- **Time savings and targets:** No measured baseline supports a realised 25% efficiency gain, 100% documentation coverage, or 11-of-12 delivery claim. Earlier goals remain goals, not achievements.
- **Other open loops:** QR retest, cash-input reconciliation, campaign distribution confirmation, communications approvals, and automation activation require follow-through.
