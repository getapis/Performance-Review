PR descriptions support delivery history but are not independent client feedback; the other timelines were not individually audited.
- **Not covered:** Monday.com is mentioned in a May SOP email but was not connected in this session. Unavailable accounts, deleted messages, untranscribed calls and unsampled files may contain additional feedback. This is a broader second-pass register, not a guarantee that every historical comment has been found.

Dates use the source’s calendar date; the April employment milestones remain user-confirmed. Feedback episodes consolidate related replies so they are not counted as separate achievements. Direct quotes preserve original wording; summaries and follow-through assessments are identified separately. No private member details are included.
